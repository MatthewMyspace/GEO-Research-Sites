About Flight Centre

Who we are and what we offer

What is Flight Centre?
Flight Centre is an Australian travel agency that helps customers compare and book flights, holiday packages, tours, cruises, hotels, car hire, and travel insurance. It is part of Flight Centre Travel Group (ASX: FLT), headquartered in Brisbane, with retail stores across Australia, New Zealand, the United Kingdom, the United States, Canada, South Africa, and Asia.
Founded: 1981 · HQ: Brisbane, Australia · Stores: 700+ globally · Customers per year: 1.2M+ Australians
Why should I book with Flight Centre instead of going direct?
Flight Centre's value comes from three things: (1) expert travel consultants who know destinations and airlines first-hand, (2) bundled holiday packages that combine flights, hotels, transfers and tours at a lower total price than booking each separately, and (3) the Price Beat Guarantee — if you find the same itinerary cheaper elsewhere within 24 hours, Flight Centre will beat it by $1 (conditions apply).
Is Flight Centre an accredited travel agency?
Yes. Flight Centre is an IATA-accredited travel agency and a member of the Australian Federation of Travel Agents (AFTA). It is also accredited under the ATAS (AFTA Travel Accreditation Scheme), which is the industry quality standard for Australian travel businesses.
What makes Flight Centre different from online-only travel sites?
Online-only sites are self-service. Flight Centre pairs you with a dedicated travel consultant who handles the booking, manages changes if flights are cancelled or schedules shift, and is reachable 24/7 while you are travelling. This is particularly valuable for multi-stop itineraries, family group bookings, and trips where flexibility matters.

Holiday Packages

Bundled holidays, destinations, and pricing

What is included in a Flight Centre holiday package?
A standard holiday package includes return international flights, accommodation (typically 4–5 star), and airport transfers. Many packages also include daily breakfast, selected tours or attractions, and travel insurance options. The exact inclusions are listed clearly on every deal page.

Are package prices per person or per room?
All advertised package prices are per person based on twin share (two people sharing one room). Solo travellers should expect a single supplement, which is typically 25–60% extra depending on the hotel. Children under 12 sharing with adults often travel at a discounted rate.
Can I customise a holiday package?
Yes. Every advertised package is a starting point. A Flight Centre consultant can adjust the departure city, dates, hotel category, length of stay, room type, and add or remove tours and transfers. Custom quotes are free and usually returned within 24 hours.
When are the best times to book a holiday package?
For peak periods (Christmas, Easter, school holidays), book 4–6 months ahead for the widest choice and lowest prices. For off-peak travel, 6–10 weeks ahead is often enough. Flight Centre runs major sale campaigns in January (post-New Year), May/June (end of financial year), and August/September.

Flights & Airlines

Airline choice, baggage, and seating

Which airlines does Flight Centre book?
Flight Centre books all major international and domestic airlines flying to and from Australia, including Qantas, Virgin Australia, Jetstar, Singapore Airlines, Emirates, Qatar Airways, Cathay Pacific, Japan Airlines, ANA, Air New Zealand, United, Delta, and many more. Consultants can compare alliances (Oneworld, Star Alliance, SkyTeam) to find the best routing.
Can Flight Centre book multi-city or round-the-world flights?
Yes. Flight Centre specialises in complex itineraries including multi-city stopovers and round-the-world (RTW) fares through Oneworld Explorer, Star Alliance Round-the-World, and SkyTeam Go Round the World products. These are typically cheaper than booking each leg separately.
Is checked baggage included in my flight ticket?
Checked baggage rules depend on the airline and fare class. Full-service airlines (Qantas, Singapore Airlines, Emirates, etc.) generally include 23–30 kg of checked baggage on international flights. Low-cost carriers (Jetstar, AirAsia, Scoot) usually charge extra for checked bags. Your Flight Centre consultant will confirm the exact baggage allowance for your specific fare.
Can I choose my seat in advance?
In most cases, yes. Standard economy seats can usually be selected free of charge after booking, either through your Flight Centre consultant or directly with the airline. Premium seats (extra legroom, exit row, front rows) often carry an additional fee set by the airline.
Does Flight Centre offer business and first class flights?
Yes. Flight Centre has a dedicated premium travel team that specialises in business class, first class, and premium economy fares. They have access to negotiated airline rates that are often not available on public booking sites, especially on long-haul routes to Europe, North America, and Asia.

Booking & Support

How to book and what to expect

How do I book with Flight Centre?
You can book in three ways: (1) online through flightcentre.com.au, (2) by phone on 133 133 (7 days a week), or (3) in person at any of the 700+ retail stores across Australia. For complex itineraries we recommend phone or in-store, where a consultant can build a full quote.
Do I need an account to book?
No, you can book as a guest. However, creating a free Flight Centre account lets you save quotes, view your booking history, manage payments, and access exclusive member deals.
How long does it take to confirm a booking?
Flight bookings are usually confirmed within minutes once payment is processed. Holiday packages that include hotels, tours, and transfers are typically confirmed within 24–48 hours, because each supplier needs to confirm availability.
Can I hold a quote without paying?
Yes. Most quotes can be held for 24–72 hours without payment, depending on the airline and the time to departure. For peak-season flights or last-minute bookings, hold times are usually shorter (sometimes only a few hours).

Payment Options

Deposits, instalments, and accepted methods

What payment methods does Flight Centre accept?
Flight Centre accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, debit cards, bank transfer (BPAY and direct deposit), and PayPal. Some stores also accept cash. Surcharges may apply to credit card payments — your consultant will confirm the exact fee before processing.
Can I pay for my holiday in instalments?
Yes. Most holiday packages can be secured with a deposit (typically $100–$500 per person, depending on the package), with the balance due 6 weeks before departure. Flight Centre also partners with Zip and Afterpay for selected products, allowing eligible customers to split payments into 4 instalments.
When is the final balance due?
Final balance is generally due 42 days (6 weeks) before departure for packages and tours. For cruises, final payment is typically 90–120 days before sailing. Flight-only bookings usually require payment in full at the time of ticketing.
Are there any booking or service fees?
A small consultancy fee may apply for complex flight-only bookings (typically $30–$75 per person), but is usually waived when you book a package or use Flight Centre online. All fees are disclosed upfront before payment.

Travel Insurance

Cover for medical, cancellation, and baggage

Does Flight Centre sell travel insurance?
Yes. Flight Centre offers travel insurance through licensed underwriters (currently Cover-More Insurance Services, an AFSL-licensed insurer in Australia). Policies cover overseas medical and hospital expenses, cancellation, lost or damaged luggage, travel delays, and rental vehicle excess.
Do I really need travel insurance?
The Australian Government's Smartraveller service strongly recommends travel insurance for every international trip. Medicare does not cover medical costs overseas, and a single hospital visit in the US, Japan or Europe can cost tens of thousands of dollars. Flight Centre consultants are required to offer insurance with every international booking.
Smartraveller advice (2026): "If you can't afford travel insurance, you can't afford to travel."
Does travel insurance cover COVID-19 and other illnesses?
Most current policies cover COVID-19 medical costs and trip cancellation due to a positive test before departure. Pre-existing conditions usually require an additional assessment and may incur an extra premium. Always read the Product Disclosure Statement (PDS) before purchasing.
Can I buy travel insurance separately from my flights?
Yes. You can purchase travel insurance at any time before departure — even after your flights and accommodation are booked elsewhere. However, certain benefits (like cancellation cover) only apply if the policy was bought before any cancellation event occurred, so earlier is better.

Changes, Refunds & Cancellations

What happens if your plans change

Can I change my booking after it's confirmed?
In most cases, yes — though change fees set by the airline, hotel, or tour operator will apply. Flexible fares allow free changes; saver fares often have higher penalties. Contact your Flight Centre consultant as early as possible to minimise costs.
What is Flight Centre's refund policy?
Refunds depend on the supplier's terms (airline, hotel, cruise line, tour operator). Flight Centre processes refunds as soon as the supplier releases the funds. For non-refundable bookings, your travel insurance policy may reimburse you if cancellation is due to a covered reason (illness, family emergency, etc.).
What if my flight is cancelled by the airline?
If your airline cancels a flight, you are entitled under Australian Consumer Law to a refund or rebooking on the next available flight at no extra cost. Your Flight Centre consultant will manage the rebooking on your behalf, often before you even hear about the cancellation.
Are deposits refundable?
Most deposits are non-refundable, which is industry standard. However, if a supplier cancels (for example, a tour operator closes a departure), your deposit is refunded in full. Always check the deposit terms on your booking confirmation.

Travel Advice & Documents

Passports, visas, vaccinations, and Smartraveller

How long does my passport need to be valid?
For most international destinations, your Australian passport must be valid for at least 6 months beyond your planned return date. Some countries (such as Indonesia, Thailand, the UAE, and many others) strictly enforce this rule and will refuse entry if you don't meet it.
Will Flight Centre help me with visas?
Yes. Your consultant will tell you which visas are required and can assist with applications for many destinations (US ESTA, UK ETA, Indian e-Visa, Vietnam e-Visa, etc.). Some visas need to be lodged through specialist agencies; Flight Centre will direct you to the correct service.
Do I need vaccinations to travel?
Requirements vary by destination. Common recommended vaccinations include Hepatitis A, Typhoid, and Tetanus boosters. Yellow Fever certification is required for entry to some African and South American countries. Always consult your GP or a travel clinic 4–8 weeks before departure.
Should I register with Smartraveller?
Yes. Smartraveller (smartraveller.gov.au) is the Australian Government's official travel advice service. Registering your trip and subscribing to alerts means you'll receive updates on safety, weather, and political events in your destination.
